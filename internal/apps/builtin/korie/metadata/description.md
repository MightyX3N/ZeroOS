Korie is a small, fast media server for the whole household. Jellyfin's own apps play from it, so the apps on your TV and phone work as they are. Adding a folder takes seconds, and Korie adds people with PINs, age limits per person, add-ons and a Store for them.

After installing, open Korie and add your folders under Settings › Libraries. The folder you pick here shows up inside Korie as /media.

TVs on your home network find Korie by themselves. To watch away from home, turn on Share online for Korie in ZeroOS: people then sign in to their household first.
